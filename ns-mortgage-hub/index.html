Mandatory documentation required by Canadian Schedule I A-Lenders and default mortgage insurers (CMHC / Sagen / Canada Guaranty). Check off completed items as you prepare.

1. Income & Employment Verification

Official Letter of Employment (Job Letter)

Dated within 30 days, on corporate letterhead, stating permanent full-time status, job title, start date, and guaranteed salary or hourly rate.

Recent Paystubs (2–3 Consecutive Periods)

Demonstrating year-to-date (YTD) earnings, statutory CPP/EI deductions, and income tax withholdings.

CRA Notices of Assessment (NOA) & T4 Slips (Past 2 Years)

Confirming reported taxable income and verification that zero balance or tax arrears are owed to the Canada Revenue Agency.

2. Down Payment & Source of Funds (AML Compliance)

90-Day Bank & Investment Account Statements

Full consecutive 90-day history showing regular accumulation. Any large or non-payroll lump sum deposit must include written proof of source.

Official Family Gift Letter (If Applicable)

Standard lender form signed by immediate family confirming gifted funds are non-repayable with no beneficial interest retained.

FHSA / RRSP Home Buyers' Plan (HBP) Withdrawal Confirmation

Tax-free first-time homebuyer withdrawals (up to $60,000 per eligible buyer under updated federal HBP limits).

3. Identity, Credit & Closing Prerequisites

Official Credit Bureau Reports (Equifax / TransUnion)

Minimum qualifying score of 650; prime tier-1 lender rates typically require 680 to 720+.

Two Pieces of Valid Government Photo ID

Unexpired Canadian PR Card, Passport, or Nova Scotia Driver's License.

Homeowner's Property & Fire Insurance Binder

Must list the mortgage lender as first loss payee and be provided to your real estate lawyer prior to closing day.
